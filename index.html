About

What Is This Tool?

This tool simulates Nuclear Magnetic Resonance (NMR) log interpretation by converting T₂ relaxation data into key reservoir properties using simplified engineering equations.

Outputs

What the Tool Calculates

φ
Porosity
Total pore volume fraction of the rock
FFI
Free Fluid Index
Fraction of fluid that can flow under gravity
BFV
Bound Fluid Volume
Fluid held by capillary forces, immovable
k
Permeability
Ability of rock to transmit fluid flow
RQI
Reservoir Quality Index
Combined indicator of reservoir quality
